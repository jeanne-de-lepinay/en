Background

Volcanoes
and Ice Caps

I hold a PhD in Geophysics from Université de Strasbourg, with doctoral research in magnetic surveying and subsurface geological interpretation, including remote fieldwork and early science communication activities. I went on to work as a geophysicist across academia and industry, spanning geology, geothermal energy, mining, archaeology, and civil engineering.

Since 2023, I have been working as an Expedition Guide, Geologist, and Environmental Scientist aboard HX vessels, accumulating 500+ days at sea across Arctic and Antarctic itineraries. Holding all relevant maritime and wilderness certifications for high-latitude work, I specialise in making complex Earth and environmental science accessible and engaging for expedition passengers.

Climate Change

How do you feel
about climate change?

Recently (2026), I've been running a survey aboard Travel HX vessels — collecting responses from passengers on their views, feelings, and attitudes toward climate change. The survey runs in English, French, German, and Chinese.

Results are analysed using k-means clustering and PCA to identify distinct respondent profiles. The findings are published openly on a dedicated website.
